How we grade

Credit: WholeLottaVinyl grading. Six grades only — M, NM, EX, VG+, VG, G+. Vinyl and cover are graded separately; the worst criterion wins. When unsure, we grade down. Anything worse than G+ is shown as G+ with a note that it is not suitable to list.

Mint (M)
Vinyl is unplayed & cover is sealed and unopened.
Near Mint (NM)
Vinyl is unplayed — the grooves haven't developed in the tracks yet. Cover has been opened but has no rips, tears or damage at all.
Excellent (EX)
Vinyl has been played but has zero scratches throughout. Cover has small wear but no rips or tears.
Very Good Plus (VG+)
Vinyl has been played but has up to a handful of hairline scratches, none feelable. Cover has small wear and light rips, but the spine is fully visible — text clearly readable.
Very Good (VG)
Vinyl has been played, with numerous hairline and easily noticeable scratches, none feelable. Cover has heavier wear and can have splits, rips and tears. Spine is partly visible.
Good Plus (G+)
Feelable scratches that can cause skips, pops and jumps. Cover has lots of splits, rips and tears, and a non-visible spine.
